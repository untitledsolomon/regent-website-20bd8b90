
# The Operational Visibility Audit: A 15-Point Infrastructure Map

<p>Most enterprises possess <strong>high-fidelity telemetry</strong> but <strong>low-fidelity context</strong>. This audit is designed to help CTOs and Architects identify the "Visibility Gaps" in their stack—the places where data is being collected but not converted into intelligence.</p>

<div class="bg-slate-50 p-6 rounded-lg border border-slate-200 my-8">
  <h4 class="font-bold text-lg mb-4">How to Use This Audit:</h4>
  <p class="mb-4">For each point, evaluate your system on a scale of 1 (Absent) to 5 (Fully Automated). A total score below 45 indicates a significant systemic risk to operational stability.</p>
  <ul class="space-y-2">
    <li><strong>Phase 1: Ingestion & Cardinality:</strong> Are you capturing the right data?</li>
    <li><strong>Phase 2: Topology & Relationship:</strong> Do you know how things connect?</li>
    <li><strong>Phase 3: Intelligence & Action:</strong> Can your system think for itself?</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<h2>Phase 1: Ingestion & Cardinality</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>1. Trace-to-Log Correlation:</strong> Can you jump from a specific log line to its parent trace in under two clicks?</li>
  <li><strong>2. Dimensional Depth:</strong> Can you filter metrics by arbitrary dimensions (e.g., user_id, deployment_id) without pre-aggregating?</li>
  <li><strong>3. Edge Telemetry:</strong> Are you capturing performance data from the user's browser/device, or only from your servers?</li>
  <li><strong>4. Schema Awareness:</strong> Does your ingestion pipeline automatically detect and alert on breaking changes to event structures?</li>
  <li><strong>5. Data Freshness:</strong> Is your end-to-end latency from event occurrence to queryability under 10 seconds?</li>
</ul>

<h2>Phase 2: Topology & Relationship</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>6. Automated Service Mapping:</strong> Is your dependency graph updated automatically in real-time as services deploy?</li>
  <li><strong>7. Database-to-App Mapping:</strong> Can you trace a slow query back to the specific line of code that triggered it?</li>
  <li><strong>8. Infrastructure-to-Business Mapping:</strong> Is there a clear link between technical components (e.g., K8s pod) and business value (e.g., Checkout)?</li>
  <li><strong>9. Blast Radius Visibility:</strong> When a component fails, does the system automatically highlight all upstream services at risk?</li>
  <li><strong>10. Multi-Cloud/Region Topology:</strong> Can you visualize traffic flow across different regions and cloud providers in a single view?</li>
</ul>

<h2>Phase 3: Intelligence & Action</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>11. Dynamic Baselining:</strong> Are your alerts based on static thresholds (e.g., >80%) or machine-learned normal behavior?</li>
  <li><strong>12. Causal Linkage:</strong> Does the system identify the "Root Cause" event automatically, or just show a group of related alerts?</li>
  <li><strong>13. SLO-Based Alerting:</strong> Are you alerting on business outcomes (e.g., failed checkouts) rather than technical inputs (e.g., CPU)?</li>
  <li><strong>14. Automated Remediation:</strong> Can your system trigger an automated rollback or failover based on an intelligence signal?</li>
  <li><strong>15. Feedback Loop Tracking:</strong> Do you track the effectiveness of your automated responses to ensure the system is learning?</li>
</ul>
