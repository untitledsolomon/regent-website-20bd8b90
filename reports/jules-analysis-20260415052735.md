- **Category Distribution:**

## 2. Structural Patterns
- **Title Structures:**
  - Dominant: "X: Y" (e.g., "Project Meridian: Modernizing...")
  - Secondary: "Why..." (e.g., "Why Most Businesses Fail to Scale")
- **Heading Hierarchy:**
  - Most posts use 3-4 H2s and minimal H3s.
  - More technical engineering posts use a deeper hierarchy (up to 8 H2s and 5 H3s).
- **Internal Linking:**
  - Existing posts typically link to 1-2 other relevant assets.

## 3. Content Gaps & Opportunities
- **Topical Authority:** Strong on Strategy and Architecture, but less depth in "Engineering" specific to "Operational Resilience".
- **Asset Interlinking:** Currently, blog posts link to case studies, but case studies and resources could be more tightly interlinked to the blog articles.

## 4. Proposed Daily Strategy
- **Theme:** Operational Resilience
